ASO360 is a production SaaS for occupational health clinics. An ASO (Atestado de Saúde Ocupacional) is the Occupational Health Certificate required by Brazilian labor regulation, for example at hiring, termination and periodic check-ups.

## What the product does

- Management of certificates, exams, patient forms and billing.
- Automated analysis following NR-7 and PCMSO guidelines.
- Customizable PDF generation (admission, dismissal, periodic, change of risk and return to work).
- Billing reports with filters.
- Access from desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Encrypted storage of sensitive data with cloud backups, built with LGPD (Brazilian data protection law) compliance in mind.

It is a subscription product with a free trial. Details and plans are on the product website.
